# Project governance

Status: **Proposed for Human Supervisor review**

## Purpose

This document governs changes to the HAIO-GATS specification repository. It
does not govern or authorize any operational deployment.

## Authority

Paul Garrett Hugel, as the Human Supervisor and specification author, retains
final authority over the normative HAIO-GATS text until a separately approved
governance transition occurs.

Contributors, reviewers, maintainers, automated checks, AI systems, and public
votes may advise that decision. They cannot exercise the Human Supervisor's
authority or authorize implementation, deployment, publication, or risk
acceptance.

## Change classes

1. **Editorial correction:** no intended semantic effect.
2. **Clarification:** resolves ambiguity without changing required behavior.
3. **Normative change:** adds, removes, or changes a requirement, conformance
   level, test, defined term, or evidence duty.
4. **Experimental extension:** explicitly non-normative proposal for evaluation.
5. **Security correction:** addresses a weakness requiring coordinated handling.

## Proposed normative process

Normative changes should require:

1. A public proposal identifying affected requirements and compatibility.
2. A documented comment period.
3. Security, privacy, human-factors, and implementation-impact review.
4. A separate reviewer who did not author the final change.
5. A recorded Human Supervisor decision with rationale.
6. A new version when the deposited specification materially changes.
7. Preservation and linkage of superseded versions.

Approval at one lifecycle gate does not imply approval at another. Merge
approval does not imply publication approval, and publication approval does not
imply operational adoption or conformance.

## Role separation

Where practical, specification editor, security reviewer, conformance reviewer,
release preparer, and publisher should be distinct actors. Any overlap must be
disclosed with its justification, risk, and compensating control.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers and contributors must disclose material financial, employment,
vendor, implementation, certification, or research interests relevant to a
proposal.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted content must identify the accountable human contributor. AI output
is not an authorizing decision, independent review, or evidence that a claim is
true.

## Unresolved governance decisions

- Maintainer and backup-maintainer identities.
- Final contributor-IP terms.
- Required review counts and code-owner assignments.
- Appeal and dispute-resolution process.
- Emergency security-change process.
- Long-term standards-body or community governance transition.
