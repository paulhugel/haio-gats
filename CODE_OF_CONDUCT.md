# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Participants are expected to sustain a respectful, evidence-oriented community
in which people can challenge technical claims without attacking individuals.
Different disciplines, jurisdictions, risk tolerances, and lived experiences
are relevant to human-supervised AI governance.

## Expected behavior

- State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest.
- Distinguish technical findings from legal or compliance conclusions.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Welcome correction and preserve superseded reasoning for accountability.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, impersonation, and
  deliberately misleading claims.
- Do not pressure reviewers or Human Supervisors to approve operational risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct,
publication of private information, deliberate concealment of material risk,
and using project channels to facilitate unauthorized access or harmful action.

## Enforcement status

Paul Garrett Hugel is the sole maintainer and retains final authority for this
preview. No private conduct-reporting channel or enforcement procedure is
currently active; this file is not itself a reporting channel. A conduct-
reporting contact and enforcement procedure must be designated before
external conduct reporting or contributions are enabled.
